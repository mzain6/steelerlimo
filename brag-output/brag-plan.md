# Brag Plan: Steeler Limousine (new website)

## What is this app?
A five-page redesign of steelerlimousine.com, a luxury SUV and limousine service in Pittsburgh: opening loader, services, fleet explorer, about and contact, and a four-step Book Now flow, all in the crest's black and gold.

## The angle
A slow, premium product film made from **real footage of the real site** (desktop only; the user asked for no mobile scene), not mockups. Every feature gets its moment on screen, in the order a visitor would meet it: the opening screen, the hero, the story, the services, the fleet, the contact page and the booking flow. The tour is the claim: nothing is described that is not shown working.

User requests that shape the edit:
- **One single video, exactly 60 seconds**, all features in it (the `/brag` default of 15–25s is deliberately overridden).
- **The pace must feel slow.** All footage plays at natural 1.0× speed (some beats gently slower). Nothing is fast-forwarded. Where a real interaction is longer than its slot, the edit cuts to the next moment with a soft dissolve instead of speeding up.
- **No mobile version** in the film.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The loading screen, for real: the gold crest fading in with a glint, the 000→100 counter climbing, one hairline progress bar. Then the two shutters part on the Pittsburgh skyline and the headline rises. Title: "The new Steeler Limousine website".

## Key moments (the middle)
- Hero: "Arrive in black & gold." settled over the skyline; the cursor glides to the hero's "Book now" button, which nudges toward the pointer and sweeps a shine. (The homepage reservation form was removed from the site at the user's request before recording.)
- Scroll-lit statement: "Pittsburgh runs on black and gold." brightening word by word as the page scrolls.
- Services index: the cursor moves down the list and a photo glides in beside each row.
- Page transition: the black curtain with the crest sweeps over the page (the footer-flash bug that used to show here was fixed before recording).
- Services page: side-dot navigation, photos wiping open, pricing tabs, FAQ accordion.
- Fleet explorer: Mid-Size SUV ↔ XL SUV, image crossfade, the underline gliding; the photo stays beside the list.
- About and contact: "From Oakdale, to wherever.", the map, the message form.
- Book Now: validation, the four steps with typed fields, the flight-number field appearing for airport rides, the live "Your trip" summary, the animated confirmation.

## Outro / punchline
Black. The crest logo settles in with a soft gold glow, the site's own footer line "Black · Gold · Pittsburgh", then "Book your ride." (the site's own button label).

## User flow worth showing
Entry → key action → result: Book Now → "Continue" with nothing chosen (validation) → pick **Airport Transfers** (flight-number field appears only for this service) → pickup, drop-off, date, time → **XL SUV** → contact details → "Send request" → confirmation. Fictional data only (name "Jane Rider", example.com email, example flight).

## Tone
- Preset: `polished`
- Creative direction: a slow, quiet premium product film. Black and gold, Pittsburgh after dark, real footage only.
- Interpretation: restraint is the style and slowness is the feel. Long eased moves, generous holds, soft dissolves; captions are small caps labels, never shouty.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080 (footage captured at 60fps)
## Duration: 60 seconds (user-requested)

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed with sparse, motion-matched accents (polished restraint)
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (steady and clean, ~110 BPM)
- Music treatment: starts 0s, fade in ~1.2s, volume ~0.30, fades out over the last ~2.8s under the outro
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset read (`vol-12.music-cues.md`, 109.96 BPM) plus the full beat grid for 0–60s. Scene cuts are snapped to the beat grid (±0.1s); at most three major reveals are locked to strong cues (the curtain into Services, the booking confirmation, the outro logo).
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; music RMS drives a soft gold bloom behind the footage frame and the crest glow in the outro. No waveform or equalizer visuals.
- SFX posture: sparse, warm, low-HF-risk. SFX are synced to the real event timestamps recorded during capture (clicks, keystrokes, hovers) and premixed into one bed.
- Restraint rule: nothing above ~0.6 volume; no SFX on every caption.

## Storyboard (target timeline; cut points flex slightly with the real footage, always on the beat grid)

1. **Opening screen** — 0.0–6.0 — real loader footage, shutters part, headline rises. Title "The new Steeler Limousine website". Soft impact as the shutters open.
2. **Hero** — 6.0–12.55 — the settled hero, the cursor gliding to "Book now" (magnetic nudge, shine), then resting on the phone button; a slow, calm scene.
3. **Scroll-lit story** — 12.55–18.0 — slow scroll; words light up; the full sentence legible ≥1.5s before the cut.
4. **Services index** — 18.0–23.5 — cursor down the rows with the following photo; faint rollover ticks.
5. **Services page** — 23.5–33.8 — click "Services", curtain sweep (beat-locked), hero, side dots, photo wipe, pricing tab, FAQ.
6. **Fleet explorer** — 33.8–40.4 — tab switch with crossfade; photo stays beside the list.
7. **About & contact** — 40.4–44.7 — "From Oakdale, to wherever." then the contact block with the map.
8. **Book Now** — 44.7–57.3 — validation, service pick, step 2 typing with the flight field, vehicle, contact, send, confirmation. Live summary fills in throughout. Soft bell on the confirmation.
9. **Outro** — 57.3–60.0 — crest, tagline, CTA, long fade to black; music fades out. Soft bell as the logo lands.

**Music mood for this video:** polished: steady, warm, unhurried.
**Audio summary:** A quiet bed fades in under the loader, lifts when the shutters open, stays out of the way through the tour with clicks and keystrokes that match the footage, and resolves with one soft bell under the logo.

## Notes
- On-screen claims are descriptions of what the footage shows. The old site's phone-number mismatch is unresolved, so the outro deliberately shows no phone number.
- No secrets, real customer data or personal email appear. Booking demo uses fictional data.
